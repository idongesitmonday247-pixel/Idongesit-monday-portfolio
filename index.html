About

For three years, I ran my own wig-making and styling business, handling customer support, orders, scheduling, content, and delivery on my own. It taught me how to keep customers happy and a business running independently.

I hold a BSc in Marketing and I'm Google Certified in Digital Marketing & E-commerce. I've also served as Departmental Secretary General, managing agendas, minutes, and follow-ups, and worked as a clinic clerk managing appointments and records. I built my virtual assistant skills through the Digital Witch Support Community where I learned customer service, CRM, and workflow automation.

Today I focus on remote virtual assistance and customer support, backed by practice projects in Zendesk, HubSpot, Monday.com, Google Drive and Slack.
