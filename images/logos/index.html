About

For three years I ran my own wig-making and styling business, handling customer support, orders, scheduling, content and delivery by myself. It taught me how to keep customers happy and a business running without anyone checking my work.

I hold a BSc in Marketing and worked as a clinic clerk, managing appointments and records. I have also trained in virtual assistance, customer service, CRM and workflow automation with the Digital Witch Support Community.

Today I focus on remote virtual assistance and customer support, backed by practice projects in Zendesk, HubSpot, Monday.com and Slack.
